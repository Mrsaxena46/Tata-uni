About TMTC

Welcome to TMTC

Serene, natural beauty. A bounty of leafy trees, a rich diversity of flora and fauna. Warm and gracious hospitality that makes for enduring and cherished memories. A tranquil oasis, in the heart of a bustling, modern city, that provides a reflective space for leaders, as they soak in fresh perspectives unencumbered by daily distractions and prepare to lead their businesses with future-ready capabilities.

The Tata Management Training Centre (TMTC), the learning arm of Tata Group Human Resources and a highly regarded leadership development institution, offers all that and more.

Our History & Heritage

Inaugurated on January 6, 1966, the TMTC was the realization of JRD Tata's vision to foster an educational institution that would assist, cultivate, and contribute to the development of professional management for the economic development of the country.

The Centre is housed in a gracious heritage structure, designed by renowned architect George Wittet, creator of other notable landmarks such as the Gateway of India, Mumbai, and the erstwhile Prince of Wales Museum, Mumbai.

Today, the TMTC continues that legacy and heritage of deep commitment to talent development in the Tata Group.
