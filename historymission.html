History & Mission

Introduction

Inaugurated on January 6, 1966, the TMTC was the realization of JRD Tata’s vision to foster an educational institution that would assist, cultivate, and contribute to the development of professional management for the economic development of the country.

JRD Tata’s foresight also gives TMTC its distinction as the second-oldest corporate university in the world; for when it began on its journey as a provider of executive education, the only other such institution in the world was GE Corporation’s centre at Crotonville.

The TMTC first came into existence as the Tata Staff College, set up by Dr. John Matthai and Prof. Rustum Choksi in 1956. Classes were held at the Turf Club of Pune. As the work grew, it became obvious that a permanent location was required to fulfil the purpose for which the centre had been conceived.

Our Foundation

The Tata Staff College thus took shape as the Tata Management Training Centre in its current premises and was housed in a gracious heritage structure designed by renowned architect George Wittet (his other famous structures include the Gateway of India and the erstwhile Prince of Wales Museum, both in Mumbai).

The early 1960s saw the restoration of what was once a colonial-era home - much of the original layout remains the same – which then began a fresh innings as a residential learning facility.

Since its inception, TMTC has justly held a proud place as a premier learning centre for business leaders. Its commitment to leadership development has remained constant with its inspiring motto: ‘Leading in Learning’.

Recognitions & Collaborations

In recognition of its many contributions, the TMTC was awarded the ‘Golden Peacock National Training Award for 2007-08’ in the field of Training & Development.

In more recent times, the Global Council of Corporate Universities (GlobalCCU), a community of corporate university professionals worldwide, recognized TMTC with a bronze award in the ‘best corporate university - business impact’ category, at the GlobalCCU Awards ceremony held in São Paulo, Brazil, on May 8, 2019.

Over the years, the TMTC has collaborated with renowned national & international institutions, faculty, and thought leaders to craft unique and rich learning experiences for executives in group companies, as well as for government sector organizations from India and other countries.

Some examples of our engagements in the government sector include a leadership development program for senior IAS officers and “Doing Business in India” programs for the governments of Singapore and the UK.

Our Mission

  • We understand that learning is a journey, and that there are no shortcuts to change.
  • We appreciate that a deep understanding of the context of the learner is vital to the design of any capability-building solution.
  • We believe in nurturing the “Tata Way” of leadership.
  • We offer thought leadership with a focus on emergent business themes and future-ready competencies.
  • We collaborate with global best-in-class delivery partners, institutions, and faculty.
  • We provide contemporary, innovative, engaging, and outcome-led learning.
