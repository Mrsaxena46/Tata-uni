Enterprise Risk Management

Description

Participants will be able to grow together towards a broader strategic view that will prepare them to be future leaders inside the organization, bridging the gap between risk management and strategy.

Key benefits

  • Creation of an integrated framework that links risk (as deﬁned at the level of Business Units) to risk for the enterprise as well as to risk for the various functions of the organization
  • A structural view of risk management as a source of value creation, framed in terms of ﬁnancial variables

Pedagogy

A variety of pedagogies will be used to engage participants, such as case analysis, faculty-led sessions to provide concepts and frameworks and an action learning project that will be an opportunity to apply the tools learned

Senior level business managers from across functions - finance, strategy planning, supply chain, Internal audit, procurement, and risk officers, with experience of 12 plus years
