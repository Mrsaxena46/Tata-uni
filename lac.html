Leaders as Coaches

Description

The objective of the program is to equip and sensitize participants to hone their people management skills using coaching techniques with their direct reports. The program consists of four main elements:

  • Assessment
  • In-class sessions
  • Webinars
  • Capstone project

The program covers key topics such as:

  • Strategic thinking
  • Coaching skills
  • Influencing with authority
  • Listening skills
  • Stakeholder management
  • Managing crucial conversations

The faculty are experts in coaching and leadership development.
