LeadershipONE

Description

The program targets Level-4 executives across Tata Motors plants and office locations, with high-performing Level-5 executives joining after their FTSS assessment.

The LeadershipONE program spans four modules over a year, incorporating various learning methodologies such as classroom sessions, case discussions, individual and team activities, role-plays, business simulation, and psychometric tools for self-reflection. Participants are required to complete two action-learning projects—visiting a dealer, a supplier, and a community partner over 3-6 months. They also need to pass a certification/assessment, involving a written exam and a presentation of their projects to a panel of experts.
