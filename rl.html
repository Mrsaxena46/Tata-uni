Resonant Leadership - Leading with EQ

Description

The scope of the program includes the following key areas:

  • Goal Setting & Performance Management: This covers effective goal-setting and communication for alignment, performance measurement, and skills for giving feedback.
  • Understanding Team Members as Individuals, Motivation, and Delegation: This involves understanding the different aspects of motivation and how individuals vary, including situational leadership and effective delegation techniques.
  • Belbin and Building High-Performing Teams: Half a day spent on understanding Belbin’s team roles and how to integrate and build high-performing teams. It also includes coaching and guiding as a manager with larger teams.
  • Collaboration & Influence: Focuses on working with different stakeholders, building and sustaining effective relationships, and influencing within the organization.

The program faculty includes experts in leadership.
