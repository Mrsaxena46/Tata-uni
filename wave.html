Waves Of Growth - HR Capability Development

Description

The key objective is to take our intact HR team on a 'reimagined learning journey' on capability. The program covers 12 HR capability areas, built on a self-directed, democratic, and non-hierarchical learning model, delivered directly to phones/desktops.

The program consists of best-in-class content and speakers on 12 high-impact HR topics chosen by the participants.

The design of the program is structured on substance, style, strategy, and sensibility, covering topics such as:

  • Workforce planning
  • High-performance teams
  • Labour & Employee Relations
  • Leveraging HR Tools & Technologies
  • Storytelling & Communication
  • Strengthening a personal brand
  • Design thinking
  • Decoding Financial Statements
  • HR Metrics & Reporting
  • Analytics & Data Science
  • Diversity & Inclusion
  • Negotiation & Influencing
  • New age psychometrics
  • Millennial workforce multipliers

SHRM is the content partner for this program, with faculty comprising external facilitators, experts, and Tata HR leaders.
