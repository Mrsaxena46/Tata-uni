Ethics Masterclass

Description

This program aims at strengthening the knowledge and skill base of participants in various aspects of interpreting guidance from the TCoC in deployment of Leadership in Business Ethics (LBE) practices. The aim is to build awareness, develop understanding, and strengthen the capability to interpret and practically apply the learning in the business context.

Attending this program will help Ethics practitioners

  • Build contemporary and relevant knowledge for the effective administration of the TCoC and LBE Action plan within their company
  • Acquire skills to promote ethical practices in the company, and de-risk the business entity from ethical violations/transgressions
  • Build their conﬁdence and capabilities while executing their respective roles as Chief Ethics Counsellors, Location Ethics Counsellors, Divisional/ Regional Ethics
  • Learn the essential aspects of key business functions
  • Understand the interrelationship between different functions — Understand the implications on business of decisions made by each managerial function
  • Develop competencies and skills essential for mid- management roles

Pedagogy

From building one's Awareness and strengthening interpretation of the TCoC & its integral connect to Brand TATA, to applying this knowledge and skill in facilitating the execution of the LBE plan integrated with business contexts.
