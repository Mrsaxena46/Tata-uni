Masterclass on Investigation

Description

Participants will go back equipped with knowledge and investigative skills that will enable them to investigate cases related to violations of TCoC-2015 (acts and omissions).

The program will sensitise delegates about

regulatory frameworks ;disciplinary procedures ; vigil mechanisms; whistle-blower and related policy deployment; work place harassment-including sexual harassment of women (POSH) in the workplace; anonymous reporting; the importance of corroborating evidence

Pedagogy

The program will be delivered in workshop mode, in the spirit of encouraging “learning by doing”.

Contact

Radha Ganesh Ram (rram@tata.com)

Who should attend

Employees who will benefit from this program are members of various ethics committees in companies–Company Ethics Counsellors (CEC), Ethics Counsellors (EC), Ethics/POSH committee members, Local/ Divisional Ethics Counsellors (LEC/DEC), HR, Finance, Internal Audit, Risk and Legal professionals, who are routinely called upon to undertake investigations in our operating companies.

Keywords

Investigation, Interviewing techniques; report writing, record keeping, anonymous reporting
