Program Goals

  • Appreciate the implications of the macroeconomic environment on business
  • Understand various business function levers that drive business performance and their interdependence
  • Analyse the business environment to create the sources of competitive advantage
  • Realize how competitive advantage manifests into business and corporate strategy
  • Gain a better understanding of self, emotions, behaviours and its impact at the work place.

Pedagogy

This is a 13-day seminar and covers core business function inputs such as Macroeconomics, Finance, Operations, Marketing, Innovation and Leadership principles and practices. The pedagogy is an interesting blend of group discussions, lectures, case studies, presentations and role play. The format allows participants to understand various management concepts and frameworks and their application in real world business situations.

Target audience:

The seminar is valuable for hi-potential employees: - who have just moved into general management roles or may move into such roles in the near future - who have substantial people responsibility - specialists in business functions or industry domains, who need a deeper understanding of inter-dependencies of business functions
