LeadershipPLUS

Description

The program is targeted at Level-3 executives across Tata Motors plants and office locations, with high-performing executives transitioning from Level-4 into Level-3 after their MCAT assessment being part of the intended target audience.

The LeadershipPLUS program is offered in 3 modules spread over 6-8 months as a residential program at the Tata Management Training Centre, Pune. Program participants engage in classroom case-based discussions, individual and team activities, role-plays, theatre playback, psychometric tool-based guidelines for self-reflection, and a live action-learning project. Additionally, participants are offered a 1:1 executive coaching session over 3 months for behavioural change and business impact.
