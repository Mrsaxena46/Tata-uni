TGELS Leadership Program

Description

A 10-day residential TGELS in partnership with Ross School of Business, University of Michigan, is ideal for senior business executives who either report to functional heads or BU Heads (and are likely to move into such roles soon) or are already reporting to CXOs in companies. The responsibility, at this level, is to collaborate with other functions to set and implement functional strategy.

The seminar journey covers the following core knowledge areas that are delivered by best in class international faculty:

Learning Goals

  • Building Capabilities for New Business Models
  • Understanding Self & Influencing Others
  • Financial Acumen for Decision Making
  • Customer Focused Marketing
  • Competitive Strategy & Advantage
  • Strategic HR Agenda

Pedagogy

An integral part of the design of the program is an action learning project that encourages participants to take up worthy challenges / opportunities that they could either lead or collaborate to solve.

Faculty

Professors from the Ross School of Business, Michigan, Cornell University, and TMTC.
