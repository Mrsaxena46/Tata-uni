Learning Goals Program

Learning Goals

Enhancing focus on creating sustainable and proﬁtable businesses, aligned with Tata Values Learning about new and innovative business models Stimulating and challenging the participants to think anew about their roles as strategic and global leaders Enhancing the ability to take a holistic approach towards thinking about business strategy Understanding the nuances of inspirational leadership

Pedagogy

Combination of case methodology, faculty-led discussions and peer learning Interaction with group leadership at the apex level
