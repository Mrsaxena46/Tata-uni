My Patterns - 360 Degree Leadership Assessment

Description

The broad objectives of the My Patterns, 360-degree view include:

  • A powerful tool for learning and leadership development
  • Enabling a culture of trust and openness in providing authentic feedback
  • Supporting the development of future engaged attributes like influence, agility, collaboration, empathy, and purpose

The 4-step process for a 360-degree view includes:

  1. Identification or nomination of leaders for assessment
  2. Identification of raters and the multi-rater assessment launch
  3. Feedback from raters and individual report generation
  4. Coaching and shortlisting key areas for development planning

The assessments are conducted on an online leadership platform to gather stakeholder ratings on competencies. It ensures anonymous data collection and reporting, with detailed reports offering multiple inference points and qualitative data.

Our partner for My Patterns is DDI (Development Dimensions International), a global leader in leadership assessment spanning across 93 countries.
