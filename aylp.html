Achieve Your Leadership Potential (AYLP)

Description

The AYLP was designed to equip participants with the skills and attitude required to manage scale and complexity, while enhancing people leadership capabilities. The program covers the following learning areas:

  • Building a Strategic Mindset
  • Enhancing Financial Acumen
  • Understanding and Leveraging Data Analytics
  • Developing Customer Centricity
  • Gaining Self-Awareness
  • Building & Leading High-Performance Teams

This intervention is delivered over several months through a blend of pedagogies, including in-class sessions, webinars, psychometric-based coaching, and action learning projects.
